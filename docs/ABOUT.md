# About icedLiquid, its author and the companies behind it

icedLiquid is a voice assistant that runs in one browser tab, built on
LFM3-Omni by [Liquid AI](https://www.liquid.ai). This page says who built it
and why.

## The author

**Walid Boudabbous** is the Chief Technology Officer of Dudoxx UG and the
founder of Acceleate Consulting. He works from Hamburg, Germany, with
professional ties to Paris, France.

He has more than 28 years of work in software, AI, healthcare, neobanking and
enterprise e-commerce. At Dudoxx he owns the technology strategy and the
delivery of the Dudoxx FHIR Clinic Platform. His applied-AI work centres on
deploying, integrating and operating LLM, RAG and speech systems, including
self-hosted inference and agent actions that need human approval.

He trained as an engineer at École Centrale de Lyon (engineering degree and
DEA, 1996).

## Dudoxx UG

[Dudoxx UG](https://www.dudoxx.com) is an AI healthcare software company in
Hamburg, Germany, founded in 2023. Its co-founders are Dr. Frenz Henning Ohm
(CEO), Dr. Nesrine Ben Anaya (COO) and Walid Boudabbous (CTO).

Dudoxx builds the **Dudoxx FHIR Clinic Platform**, an AI-first clinic
management system:

- one platform with role-based portals for doctors, staff, administrators
  and patients;
- a FHIR R4 clinical core, so clinic data is not locked into one vendor;
- self-hosted AI: speech-to-text, text-to-speech and LLM inference run on
  the clinic's own servers or in a managed EU cloud;
- voice intake, clinical documentation, scheduling, billing and
  telemedicine around that core.

icedLiquid is a Dudoxx R&D project. It tests how much of a voice assistant
one small open model can carry on local hardware, with no inference server.

Contact: contact@dudoxx.com
