# Acceleate Consulting

## About Acceleate

Acceleate Consulting is a European consulting firm for AI innovation, digital
health and fintech, based in Tallinn, Estonia. Its tagline is "Where AI Vision
Meets Execution". Walid Boudabbous is its founder and principal technology
consultant.

Source: https://www.acceleate.com

## Work domains

Acceleate works in four domains. AI: custom language-model development,
retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), predictive analytics and AI automation.
Healthcare digitalization: physician-led AI adoption, medical documentation
systems and telemedicine. Fintech: digital banking platforms, payment
processing and blockchain. Enterprise: ERP AI integration, enterprise
architecture and cloud solutions.

## Research and development

Acceleate runs applied R&D on self-hosted speech and language models, on-device
inference and voice agents. icedLiquid is one result of that work: a voice
assistant that runs Liquid AI's LFM3-Omni model fully in the browser on WebGPU.
