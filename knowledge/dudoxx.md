# Dudoxx

## About Dudoxx

Dudoxx UG is an AI-enabled healthcare software company based in Hamburg, Germany.
It builds the Dudoxx FHIR Clinic Platform, an AI-first healthcare management
system for clinics and practices. Dudoxx is the company behind icedLiquid.

Source: https://www.dudoxx.com

## Founders and team

Dudoxx was founded by Dr. Frenz Ohm (CEO), Dr. Nesrine Ben Anaya (COO), both
dermatologists, and Walid Boudabbous (CTO). The company was registered in
Hamburg in November 2023.

## The Dudoxx FHIR Clinic Platform

The platform brings electronic health records, scheduling, billing, dictation,
document AI and a patient portal together around one FHIR clinical core. It
offers role-based portals for doctors, staff, administrators and patients on
one platform. It can run in a managed EU cloud or on the clinic's own servers,
with self-hosted AI inference, so patient data can stay on site.

## Products and modules

Dudoxx products include an agentic clinical AI orchestrator, voice-first
patient intake, a document intelligence platform that turns documents into
searchable knowledge, a suite of real-time medical voice agents, and
self-hosted telemedicine.

## Technology

Dudoxx builds on FHIR R4 and R5, PostgreSQL, Redis, the Qdrant vector
database, NestJS, Next.js and React, with self-hosted speech-to-text,
text-to-speech and language-model inference behind an OpenAI-compatible
gateway.
